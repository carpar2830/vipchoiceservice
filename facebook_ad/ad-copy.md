## 2. Campaign Setup (Meta Ads Manager)

| Setting | Recommendation | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Objective** | **Leads** or **Engagement → Messages** | Best for getting actual contacts, not just views |
| **Conversion location** | Instant Form (Leads) or Messenger | Keeps people in-app = cheaper leads |
| **Budget** | Start **$10–$20/day** | Enough to learn, low risk; scale what works |
| **Schedule** | Run 7+ days before judging | Meta needs ~50 leads to optimize |
| **Placements** | Manual: **Reels + Stories + Feed** (FB & IG) | The creative is built 9:16 for these |

### Targeting (the key to "great local results")
- **Location:** Dallas, TX + a **10–15 mile radius** (tighten to 5 mi if budget is small).
- **Age:** 25–64 (adults who file taxes / own businesses).
- **Detailed targeting (optional — Meta often does better with broad):**
  - Interests: *Small business owners, Entrepreneurship, Self-employed,
    QuickBooks, Tax preparation, Real estate*
  - Behaviors: *Small business owners*
- **Tip:** With a small daily budget, **broad targeting + tight radius** usually
  beats lots of narrow interests. Let the creative do the filtering.

### Lead Form fields (keep it short!)
Name · Phone · Email · "What do you need help with?" (Tax / Bookkeeping /
Notary / Business setup). Fewer fields = more leads.

## 3. Best Practices for This Ad

- ✅ **Captions carry it** — 80%+ watch on mute. Text is on every slide already.
- ✅ **Strong hook in first 3 seconds** — the "Stop overpaying the IRS" frame.
- ✅ **One clear offer + one CTA** — the free consult. Don't dilute it.
- ✅ **Respond fast** — local leads go cold in hours. Reply within minutes.
- ✅ **Test 2 versions** — run Option A vs Option B copy, keep the winner.
- ✅ **Refresh every 2–3 weeks** — swap the hook so it doesn't go stale.

## 4. Timely angles by season (swap the hook slide to match)
- **Jan–Apr:** "Tax season — get your maximum refund."
- **May–Jun:** "Q2 estimated taxes due — don't get penalized." / "Mid-year books cleanup."
- **Jul–Sep:** "Starting a business this summer? Set up your LLC right."
- **Oct:** "Extension deadline Oct 15 — file before the penalty."
- **Nov–Dec:** "Year-end tax planning — save before Dec 31."
