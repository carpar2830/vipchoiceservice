# VIP Choice Service — Project Notes

Tax preparation, bookkeeping, notary, and business services website and
marketing assets for Dallas, TX.

## Marketing asset preferences

These apply to all reels, ads, and video/audio mockups going forward:

### Music / audio
- **No constant, repetitive, or busy backing track.** It distracts from the ad.
- Use a **soft, calming "healing" tone** — gentle, warm, low in the mix.
- **Vary the music** — avoid a single looping note/phrase; let it evolve subtly
  so it stays pleasant and never monotonous.
- Audio must **never compete with or distract from the actual ad message**.
- Default any sound to **off with a clear play/mute toggle** (browsers block
  autoplay-with-sound anyway), and keep the volume gentle.
